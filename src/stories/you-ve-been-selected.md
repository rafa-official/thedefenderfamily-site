** see June 8th 2026 update below **

I made it. This weekend I’ll be joining the Defender Trophy Spain Qualifiers. Just getting here already feels like a victory. (Only 1% of applicants make it this far.)

As a kid in the early 90s, I had Camel Trophy posters covering my bedroom walls. (See the picture of mu brother in my bed) Back then, I thought adventure was about vehicles, remote places, and crossing rivers.

Life taught me otherwise.

![camel trophy poster boy in the 90s](/images/uploads/camel-trophy-poster-90.jpg)

The Defender Family was never built to chase followers or fame. It started with a family, an old Defender, a camera, and a curiosity for the world.

Life took me across continents, through deserts, mountains, and remote communities. It also took me from those places to photographing some of the world's biggest celebrities at its most prestigious events. Along the way, I worked with NGOs, co-founded @realitytelling with my wife, and learned that adventure is not only about where you go, but about the positive impact you leave behind.

![rafa lanus ngo photographer](/images/uploads/NGO-rafa.jpg)
![hombre leyendo atrapa tus sueños](/images/uploads/atrapa-tus-suenos.jpg)
![driving in tanzania](/images/uploads/rafa-diver-tanzania.jpg)

Some of my most important journeys happened far from any expedition. A bad kidney tumor. A phone call telling me my son had suffered a severe motorcycle accident and was fighting for his life in an ICU thousands of kilometers away.

> "Life taught me otherwise."

![defender family tough moments in hospitals](/images/uploads/rafa-hopsital.jpg)
![defender family tough moments in hospitals](/images/uploads/oggito-hospital.jpg)

Those experiences taught me that adventure is not the absence of fear.

It is moving forward despite it.

It is adapting when the plan falls apart.

It is carrying responsibility when the outcome is uncertain.

![man checking map of africa](/images/uploads/rafa-map-TDF.jpg)
![rafa lanus ngo photographer](/images/uploads/rafa-pallapupas.jpg)
![rafa lanus ngo photographer](/images/uploads/rafa-ngo-video.jpg)

Defender for me is not a logo, a kicka** vehicle, or a brand.

Defender is a verb.

To defend what matters the most.

Your family, your team, your mission, your character.

> "Defender is a verb"

I don’t know what will happen this weekend.

But one thing is already true.

Rafita, the kid with the Camel Trophy posters would be smiling.

And so am I.

Thank you to all of those who supported.

![Land Rover Defender in the desert in Spain](/images/uploads/defender-desert.jpg)
![Man happy driving his Land Rover Defender](/images/uploads/rafa-happy-defender-driving.jpg)

*** UPDATE - June 08, 2026 ***

So... how did it go? Did I win?

A lot of people have been asking.

If by winning you mean, "Was I selected to represent Spain at the Defender Trophy Final in South Africa?", then no. That honor goes to Max Ferrer, and I genuinely wish him the very best. He's an incredible guy, and Spain will be very well represented.

![Land Rover Defender Brand Ambasador](/images/uploads/rafa-lanus-defender-ambasador.jpg)
![defender trophy happy moments in spain](/images/uploads/rafa-lanus-defender-trophy-2026.jpg)

But if winning means spending one of the best weekends of your life, meeting extraordinary people with inspiring stories, learning from true Camel Trophy legends, pushing yourself, laughing, struggling, and realizing you've found a community that shares your values...

Then yes.

> "I WON"

I met people that I know will become lifelong friends. People who understand that adventure is about curiosity, teamwork, resilience, and helping each other when things get difficult.

![defender family with the nextmeridian expedition nick and mathilde](/images/uploads/defender-family-nextmeridian-expedition.jpg)
![Land Rover Defender Camel Trophy](/images/uploads/defender-trophy-spain-2026-driving.jpg)
![defender trophy happy moments in spain](/images/uploads/defender-trophy-spain-2026-offroad-driving.jpg)

I'd also like to thank everyone at @defender.

I expected an incredibly professional event. What I wasn't sure I was going to find was the warmth, kindness, and genuine human connection everyone brought to it. That says a lot about a company building something truly special.

![camel trophy legend south africa](/images/uploads/camel-trophy-legend-south-africa.jpg)
![defender trophy staff and participants](/images/uploads/defender-trophy-spain-2026.jpg)
![defender trophy spanish competitors 2026](/images/uploads/defender-trophy-spain-team.jpg)

I keep coming back to the last picture in this post.

Griff from the Defender team walked over, pulled out his phone and said, "Come on, we need a picture of this."

We had just won one of the challenges together, and at the time it felt like nothing more than a fun moment between teammates.

In that frame are Mathilde, who would go on to win the French selection, Max, who will represent Spain in South Africa, and me.

![defender trophy european finalist 2026](/images/uploads/defender-trophy-finalist-europe-2026.jpg)

If you ask me whether I won...

I think that picture answers the question better than I ever could.

This weekend feels less like the end of a competition and more like the beginning of a whole new chapter for The Defender Family.

![defender trophy happy moments](/images/uploads/defender-ambasador-for-family-travel.jpg)

> "A whole new chapter for The Defender Family."

As said before, Defender is a verb. 

It's about defending what matters. 

And this weekend, I got to meet a whole community of people who live by it.
